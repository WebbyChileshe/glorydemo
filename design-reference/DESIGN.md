## Brand & Style

This design system embodies compassionate stewardship, dignified sanctuary, and vibrant human potential. Created for an orphanage and child empowerment foundation, it departs from pity-driven tropes in favor of an uplifting, editorial narrative rooted in warmth, safety, and luminous optimism. 

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Modernism** with **Organic Tactility**:
- Rich, grounding pine slate tones communicate permanence, institutional integrity, and shelter.
- Radiant sunset amber and golden ochre accents evoke warmth, morning sun, and unbridled hope.
- Organic circular masking, floating overlapping vignettes, and subtle hand-drawn accents give the interface a bespoke, story-led personality without sacrificing structural clarity.
- Typography blends human-scale warmth with refined modernism, presenting compelling stories, transparent impact metrics, and intuitive donation pathways with graceful authority.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of quiet refuge and welcoming radiance:
- **Primary (`#1B3B36` - Deep Pine Sanctuary):** A deep, saturated evergreen slate that serves as the predominant canvas, conveying serenity, heritage, and protective shelter.
- **Secondary (`#F2A93B` - Sunset Amber / Golden Ochre):** The vital energetic anchor, used for primary call-to-actions, active indicators, and radiant focal borders around imagery.
- **Tertiary (`#FBF8F1` - Warm Alabaster):** A tender, warm-tinted off-white used for primary headlines, high-contrast text layers, and light-mode card surfaces that provide clean breathing room.
- **Neutral (`#0F2420` - Obsidian Forest):** The deepest shadow tier, used for ground layers, footers, structural dividers, and elevated backdrop contrasts.
- **Complementary Accents:**
  - *Muted Sage (`#3D6B63`)* for borders, pill chips, and subtle secondary iconography.
  - *Sunlit Sand (`#F5E6CC`)* for secondary body text on dark surfaces, offering softness without eye strain.

## Typography

The typography couples the sculptural, editorial presence of **Epilogue** with the humane, legibility-first cadence of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:
- **Epilogue** commands large narrative moments, hero proclamations, and section headers. Its distinctive proportions impart an authentic, published feel reminiscent of high-end philanthropic journalism.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a friendly, warm, and hyper-legible companion for descriptive body copy, financial breakdowns, and microcopy.
- Headlines maintain a tight line height to deliver punchy, emotional resonance, while body copy enjoys generous vertical breathing room (`1.6` to `1.7` ratio) for effortless reading on both light and deep pine surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The foundation utilizes a **12-column fluid grid** centered within a `1280px` maximum viewport container:
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` margins. The hero layout leans into asymmetrical, staggered two-column compositions (7 columns for mission copy and donation actions; 5 columns for the overlapping circular photo cluster).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, gutters scaled to `1.25rem`, margins at `2rem`. Overlapping visual vignettes scale proportionally and settle into vertical arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Clustered circular vignettes transition to a stacked card with horizontal scroll or a single hero circle with orbiting micro-thumbnails.
- Rhythm flows from an 8pt base unit. Layout components enforce ample negative space around impact figures and stories to let imagery and emotionally resonant copy take center stage.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an ambient interplay of **tinted tonal translucency** and **soft warm glows**:
- **Base Surface:** Dark Pine (`#1B3B36`) forms the primary ground plane.
- **Elevated Cards & Containers:** Styled with a 10% to 15% opacity white or sage overlay (`rgba(251, 248, 241, 0.07)`), blurred with an 8px to 12px backdrop filter and bordered by a delicate 1px border (`rgba(251, 248, 241, 0.12)`).
- **Amber Accent Elevation:** Active donation buttons and highlight modules cast a rich, warm shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(242, 169, 59, 0.35)`), reinforcing physical tactility and invitation.
- **Image Stacking:** Circular image frames leverage multiple layered z-indices with contrasting 2px to 4px golden ochre borders (`#F2A93B`), establishing clear spatial separation over dark backdrops without harsh drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry to convey approachability, care, and continuous movement:
- **Buttons & Chips:** Full pill radii (`9999px`) provide inviting touch points that feel soft and friendly.
- **Cards & Story Containers:** Curated with `rounded-xl` (`2rem` / `32px`) radii, providing soft architectural containers for sensitive stories.
- **Hero & Feature Imagery:** Employs circular masking (`border-radius: 50%`) and organic pebble-shaped masks. Clustered circles overlap playfully, adorned with decorative hand-drawn SVG flourishes (sunbursts, curved arrows, and joy-burst lines) to reflect childlike wonder and hope.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Donate Now", "Sponsor a Child"):** High-contrast Amber (`#F2A93B`) pill background with obsidian forest text (`#0F2420`, font-weight 700). Subtle scale on hover (`scale(1.02)`) accompanied by an enhanced warm ambient glow.
- **Secondary CTA ("Watch Video", "Our Mission"):** Transparent pill container with a 1.5px warm alabaster border (`#FBF8F1`), featuring an integrated circular play icon button with a micro-gap.
- **Compact Donation Tiers:** Quick-select donation pills ($25, $50, $100, Custom) that switch from transparent muted pine to solid golden ochre upon selection.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges ("Happy Childhood", "850+ Children Housed"):** Compact, pill-shaped tags featuring 1px sage borders (`rgba(242, 169, 59, 0.3)`), subtle warm backdrops, and uppercase tracking to introduce hero headlines.

### Cards & Story Highlights
- **Impact Cards:** Translucent dark pine surfaces, `2rem` rounded corners, thin 1px ghost borders, equipped with organic photo cutouts, progress bars (golden amber fill on dark sage track), and verified charity badges.
- **Donor Trust Tiers:** Subtle, monoline partner lockups (e.g., Forbes, BCA, PayPal, Visa) presented at 60% opacity warm alabaster with hover states returning to 100%.

### Form Inputs & Radios
- **Donation Frequency Toggle:** Segmented pill toggle ("One-time" vs "Monthly") with seamless sliding amber background.
- **Text Inputs:** Soft alabaster background with deep pine typography, featuring generous 1rem internal padding and crisp focus rings in sunset amber.

### Illustrative Flourishes
- **Doodle Elements:** Hand-drawn line art (dynamic directional arrows, radiant sun rays, and organic underlines) rendered in pure `#FBF8F1` or `#F2A93B` with stroke-widths of 2px to 2.5px, providing authentic human warmth alongside pristine typography.
